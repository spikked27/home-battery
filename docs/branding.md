# Brand assets

Home Battery ships transparent PNG icons and logos in `custom_components/home_battery/brand/`, served locally by Home Assistant 2026.3+ and used by the dashboard and HACS. Light/dark assets use the same readable mark. The source artwork was generated using the built-in image generation tool; packaging resizes it to 256 and 512 pixels while preserving alpha.

Production prompt: "Create a professional square app icon for Home Battery, a local Home Assistant battery energy management integration. Single memorable geometric emblem integrating the outline of a house with a battery and a subtle lightning bolt in its negative space. Flat crisp vector-like design, generous bold forms legible at 48 pixels, balanced centered silhouette, teal and sky-blue accents with a small amber energy accent consistent with an energy dashboard. No text, no letters, no mockup, no gradients, no shadows, no photographic effects. Transparent background, ample small safe margin around the symbol."

Brand images follow the [Home Assistant local brand image convention](https://developers.home-assistant.io/docs/core/integration/brand_images/) and [HACS brand requirements](https://www.hacs.xyz/docs/publish/integration/).
